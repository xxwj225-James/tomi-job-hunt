# Legal Disclaimer & Compliance Design

TomiHunt is a **local-first efficiency tool**. Understanding its legal
posture requires understanding what it is — and what it deliberately is not.

## What TomiHunt is

1. **A user-side browser enhancement.** It parses the DOM of pages *you are
   already viewing* in *your own browser*. It never crawls, batches, or
   otherwise accesses third-party platforms' servers on your behalf.
2. **A local data processor.** All AI processing, tagging, storage and search
   happen on your machine. The Core service binds to `127.0.0.1` only.
3. **A neutral protocol for structured, user-generated content.** The only
   data that can ever be shared (future community-feed phases) is
   *structured evaluations* produced by users — enum-based fact tags and
   sanitized notes. Raw JD text, HR names and contact details are excluded
   **by construction** (`buildSharedIntel` in `core/src/jd/sanitize.ts`).
4. **A human-in-the-loop messenger.** TomiHunt **never sends a message
   automatically**. AI-drafted pitches are filled into the chat box and
   highlighted; the user reviews and sends them with their own Enter / click.
   There is no code path that dispatches a message without the user's own
   action (auto-send was removed in 2026 for this reason).

## What TomiHunt is not

- Not a scraper, crawler, or data-mining tool for third-party platforms.
- Not a publisher of job listings or platform content.
- Not operated as a service: there are no servers, no accounts, no content
  moderation team producing content.

## User responsibility

Users must comply with the terms of service of the websites they visit and
with the laws applicable where they live. Sharing any information (including
community-feed contributions) is the user's own act; users are responsible
for the accuracy and lawfulness of what they submit.

## Compliance safeguards built into the code

| Safeguard | Where |
|---|---|
| Local-only processing; telemetry strictly opt-in & OFF by default | `core/src/index.ts` binds 127.0.0.1; `docs/privacy.md`, `docs/telemetry.md` |
| PII masking (phones, emails, WeChat) | `core/src/jd/sanitize.ts` |
| Abusive-language neutralization + 100-char clamp | `core/src/jd/sanitize.ts` |
| Structured enum reports instead of free text | `core/src/jd/schema.ts` |
| Shared intel excludes raw JD text by construction | `core/src/jd/sanitize.ts` (`buildSharedIntel`) |
| Request rate limiting for LLM calls | `core/src/queue.ts` (concurrency-limited FIFO) |
