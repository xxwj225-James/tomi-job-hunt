# Takedown Policy (通知-删除)

TomiHunt itself does not host user content. Community-shared content (future
phases) lives on third-party infrastructure (GitHub Issues/Discussions,
Nostr relays, or static feeds) under the accounts and keys of the users who
submitted it.

## If you believe content infringes your rights

If you are a company or individual and believe that community-shared content
in a TomiHunt feed or repository is false, defamatory, or infringes your
rights:

1. Open an issue in this repository titled `Takedown request: <summary>`, or
2. Contact the maintainers at the email listed in the repository.

Include:
- The exact URL or feed entry of the content in question
- The specific factual claims you dispute
- Your name, organization, and official proof of authority to act (e.g.
  business license scan)

## What happens next

- Maintainers will acknowledge within 48 hours.
- Where the content lives in infrastructure we control (this repository's
  issues, files, or feeds we generate), we will remove or quarantine the
  disputed entry within 24 hours of a valid request (notice-and-takedown,
  consistent with safe-harbor provisions such as Article 1195 of the PRC
  Civil Code).
- Where the content lives on third-party infrastructure we do not control
  (GitHub repositories of other users, Nostr relays), we will point you to
  that platform's own abuse-report process.

## Scope

This policy covers content we can administratively act on. It does not cover
AI-generated text produced privately on a user's own machine, which never
touches any shared infrastructure.
